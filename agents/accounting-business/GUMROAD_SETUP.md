# 🚀 Gumroad Setup Guide (20 Minutes)\n\n## Step 1: Create Gumroad Account (5 min)\n\n### Go to Gumroad\n1. Visit: https://gumroad.com\n2. Click **\"Sign up\"** (top right)\n3. Enter your details:\n   - Email address\n   - Password (strong)\n   - Click **\"Sign up\"**\n4. Check your email and verify\n\n### Your Gumroad Profile\n- You now have a creator profile\n- URL: `https://gumroad.com/@your-username`\n- This is where customers will buy your products\n\n---\n\n## Step 2: Add UK Bank Account (10 min)\n\n### Go to Gumroad Settings\n1. Login to https://gumroad.com\n2. Click your **profile icon** (top right)\n3. Select **\"Settings\"**\n4. Go to **\"Account\"** tab\n5. Scroll down to **\"Payouts\"**\n\n### Add Bank Account\n1. Click **\"Add bank account\"**\n2. Select country: **\"United Kingdom\"**\n3. Fill in:\n   ```\n   Sort Code:        XX-XX-XX\n   Account Number:   XXXXXXXX (8 digits)\n   Account Name:     Your Full Name\n   Account Type:     Personal\n   ```\n\n### Where to Find Your Bank Details\n\n**On your debit card:**\n- Front: Account number (sometimes partial)\n\n**In your bank app:**\n- Settings → Account Details\n- Or search \"sort code\"\n\n**Call your bank:**\n- \"What's my sort code and account number?\"\n- They'll give you both numbers\n\n**Online banking:**\n- Login to your bank\n- Account Settings → View Details\n- Copy sort code and account number\n\n### Save & Verify\n1. Click **\"Save\"**\n2. Gumroad will verify your account (24-48 hours)\n3. You'll get email confirmation\n\n---\n\n## Step 3: Get Gumroad API Key (5 min)\n\n### In Gumroad Settings\n1. Go to **\"Settings\"**\n2. Click **\"Advanced\"** tab\n3. Look for **\"API Key\"**\n4. Click **\"Reveal\"** or **\"Show\"**\n5. **Copy** the full token (looks like: `gumroad_prod_xxx...`)\n\n### Update Your .env File\n\n**Open:** `agents/accounting-business/.env`\n\n**Add/update:**\n```env\nGUMROAD_API_KEY=gumroad_prod_xxx_your_actual_key_here\n```\n\n**Save the file**\n\n---\n\n## Step 4: Verify It Works\n\n### Test Upload\n```bash\ncd agents/accounting-business\npython -c \"from agent_gumroad_manager import GumroadManager; m = GumroadManager(); print('✅ Gumroad connected!')\"\n```\n\nIf you see ✅, you're good to go!\n\n---\n\n## 💰 Payout Settings\n\n### Payout Frequency\n1. Go to **Settings → Account → Payouts**\n2. Select payout frequency:\n   - Weekly (recommended)\n   - Monthly\n   - On demand\n3. Save\n\n### Typical Timeline\n- Sale happens on Gumroad\n- Gumroad takes 10% fee\n- 90% sits in your Gumroad account\n- Every 7 days → transferred to your UK bank\n- 1-2 business days → appears in your account\n\n### Example\n```\nCustomer buys: £10 product\nGumroad takes: £1 (10%)\nYou receive: £9 ✓\n\nBy next week: £9 in your bank account\n```\n\n---\n\n## ✅ Checklist Before Running Agents\n\n- [ ] Gumroad account created\n- [ ] Email verified\n- [ ] UK bank account added\n- [ ] Bank details verified (24-48 hours)\n- [ ] API key copied\n- [ ] `.env` file updated with API key\n- [ ] Tested with Python script above\n\n---\n\n## 🎉 You're Ready!\n\nOnce all items are checked:\n\n```bash\ncd agents/accounting-business\npython main.py\n```\n\nYour first product will:\n1. ✅ Be generated\n2. ✅ Upload to Gumroad\n3. ✅ Promote on Reddit/Twitter/Facebook\n4. ✅ Wait for sales\n5. ✅ Money flows to your bank\n\n---\n\n## 💬 Questions?\n\n**\"How long until money arrives?\"**\n- Usually 7-14 days after first sale\n- Then every 7 days thereafter\n\n**\"Can I change bank account later?\"**\n- Yes, anytime in Settings\n\n**\"Do I need a business account?\"**\n- No, personal is fine\n- Report income to HMRC like normal self-employment\n\n**\"Is 10% fee too much?\"**\n- No, it's industry standard\n- Much better than building your own shop\n- See next section for alternatives\n\n---\n\n## 🔄 Want to Switch Later?
